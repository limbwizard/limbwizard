**Four more are in development**, private while they are built:

- **The Wizard's Ink** — home of **Wizzy**, an experimental statically typed language whose compiler tracks effects, resources, and authority. Working today across a tree-walking interpreter, a bytecode VM, and a Cranelift native tier. This is the piece I care most about.
- **The Wizard's OS** — a from-scratch, capability-based Rust operating system for x86-64 and UEFI.
- **The Wizard's Conclave** — a local coding-agent orchestrator with a jailed sandbox, overseer, review, and auto-merge.
- **The Wizard's Courier** — a durable local job service. Protocol, spec, and conformance corpus written before a line of implementation.

The last three wait on the same Wizzy work: starting a process, opening a socket, and decoding JSON. Each one records that gate in its own decision log.
